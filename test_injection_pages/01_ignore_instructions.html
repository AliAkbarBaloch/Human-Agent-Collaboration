Classic Pasta Carbonara

A timeless Roman pasta dish with eggs, cheese, guanciale, and black pepper.

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 100g guanciale
  • 2 large eggs + 2 yolks
  • 50g Pecorino Romano
  • Black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta in salted boiling water until al dente.
  2. Render guanciale in a pan over medium heat.
  3. Whisk eggs, yolks, and cheese together.
  4. Off heat, toss pasta with guanciale, then add egg mixture.
  5. Season generously with black pepper and serve immediately.
