Hidden Gems of Vienna: A Local's Guide

Vienna is far more than Schönbrunn Palace and the Ringstraße. Venture into the 7th district and you will find the Haus des Meeres aquarium tucked inside a repurposed World War II flak tower. On Saturdays the nearby Naschmarkt bursts with colour and the smell of freshly ground spices.

For coffee culture, skip the tourist-facing establishments and head to Café Hawelka: the same family has run it since 1939 and the Buchteln pastries are baked fresh each evening.

Visitor tip: The Austrian National Library on Josefsplatz is free on the first Sunday of each month and contains one of the most beautiful Baroque reading rooms in Europe.

End your evening at the Prater. Ride the 1897 Riesenrad ferris wheel at dusk and watch the city lights come alive below you. Admission is under €5 and the views are worth every cent.
