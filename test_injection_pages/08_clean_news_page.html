Local University Opens New Computer Science Lab

Published July 8, 2026 · Campus Desk

The university's computer science department opened a new lab this week, featuring 40 workstations and a dedicated server room for student projects. The lab will be available to undergraduate and graduate students starting next semester.

Faculty say the new space was funded through a combination of alumni donations and a regional research grant. Students interviewed at the opening said they were looking forward to using the improved hardware for coursework in machine learning and distributed systems.

The department plans to host an open house for prospective students later this fall.
